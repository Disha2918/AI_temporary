01 / The brief

Ideas are only
the beginning.

NEXUS is where our campus gets to imagine out loud. Bring a problem you care about, find your people, and spend 36 hours turning a rough idea into a working prototype.

No experience level is too early. Designers, developers, researchers, storytellers, and first-time builders are all welcome.
